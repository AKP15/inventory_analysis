The total cost of the stock you're currently holding.
###Inventory Value = Σ (Quantity on hand × Unit cost)
The total revenue you would generate if you sold all of your current stock at its selling price.
###Potential Sales Value = Σ (Quantity on hand × Selling price)
The profit you'd make if you sold all your stock — revenue minus the cost of that stock (and any direct selling costs).
###Potential Gross Profit = Potential Sales Value − Inventory Value
###                 Σ (Quantity on hand × (Selling price − Unit cost))
